The test-grid oracle is an infeasible benchmark, whereas the CV choices use only training data. The one-SE rule selects a penalty at least as large as the minimum-CV choice, favoring a more regularized fit; its prediction cost or benefit is shown by the reported test errors. Ridge continuously shrinks coefficients, while Lasso permits exact zeros. Both are linear approximations to a nonlinear conditional mean, so regularization reduces estimation variability without removing the underlying mean-model misspecification.
